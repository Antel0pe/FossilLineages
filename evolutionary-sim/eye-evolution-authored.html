Eyes evolving — the authored run

In eye-evolution-live.html the eye has to be earned: nothing rewards it directly and you wait for selection. Here it is scripted. A single dial — evolutionary time — walks both lineages along the real morphological sequence (flat light-sensitive patch → cup → deepening pit → narrowing pinhole → lens), continuously, with no stages and no thresholds. Everything downstream is still computed, not authored: what an animal can resolve, how far off it notices, whether it eats, escapes, or finds a mate all fall out of the same optics. Watch the hunt and the escape rebuild themselves as the eye sharpens.

How they hunt · how they run

Every bar is a smooth function of acuity — there is no rule anywhere in this page that says “if the eye is good enough, start stalking”. The weights slide continuously, which is why the behaviour bleeds from one style into the next instead of switching.

The climb — Δρ over evolutionary time (log)

Lower is sharper. Dashed lines are Nilsson's class boundaries (100° I|II, 20° II|III, 2° III|IV). The two lines drift apart and cross: the arms race is authored in as a small phase lag, so whichever side is momentarily ahead is the one whose numbers are winning above.
