Robustness check — does my tuning decide the outcome? (click to run sweeps)

Each sweep re-evolves the population many times, changing one knob across orders of magnitude, and plots the final sharpness. What to look for: no sweep produces a cliff — every setting still evolves a functional lens eye, the curves just slide smoothly. That "no threshold / no categorically different outcome" is the structural stability you wanted. Notes per knob: cost (the one you worried about tuning) is nearly flat — it doesn't decide the result. Mutation rate genuinely shifts the plateau, but that's real mutation load (more mutation = the population sits looser around the peak), not modelling bias — and it's still smooth. Predator demand β barely moves sharpness because in a single predator world any real predator drives a full lens eye; β sets how much survival that eye buys, not the eye's type. Getting genuinely different eye types needs the deferred light-level pressure.
