Eyes evolving — the physics core, live

This page runs the exact same core/sim.mjs the headless experiments use — one step() per tick, drawn to the canvas. Nothing here rewards a sharp eye. Δρ (acceptance angle) is derived from the aperture/depth/lens genome; detection is p = S/(1+S) with S = (θ/Δρ)²·e^(−d/atten). Watch the mean-Δρ line fall as foraging and predation select for eyes on their own. Turn mutation up to see it in your lifetime.
