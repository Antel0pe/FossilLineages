sahara cycle between green and desert due to earth's Precession. the cycle is basically the tilt of the earth changes over some 20k year cycle. the sahara is where the air is heated up most and then moves away from equator, descends as rain then near surface blows back towards sahara after dropping all moisture. when the earth tilts, the place where air rises and returns with no moisture changes. this change means where the sahara gets air with no moisture suddenly becomes where air descends with moisture.
